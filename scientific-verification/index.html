Critical Research: Pregnancy & Child Development

One of our most important studies examines the impact of mobile phone use during pregnancy and its effects on child development.

Prenatal EMF Exposure Study
Institution:

University of Barcelona, Spain

Duration:

5-year longitudinal study

Participants:

2,847 pregnant women

Follow-up:

Children tracked to age 7

Key Findings:

  • Children exposed to high prenatal mobile phone use showed 25% higher risk of behavioral problems
  • Increased hyperactivity and attention difficulties observed at age 7
  • Mothers using EMF protection showed significantly reduced risk factors

Study Implications

This groundbreaking research highlights the critical importance of EMF protection during pregnancy. The developing brain is particularly vulnerable to electromagnetic interference, making protection essential for expectant mothers.

Protection Recommendations

  • Use mobile phone neutralizers during pregnancy
  • Place Neutralisers in front of your TV, near your microwave, & inside your fridge.
  • Wear the Neutraliser on your body for personal protection
  • Continue protection during breastfeeding and early childhood

Important Note:

This study represents one of the largest longitudinal investigations into prenatal EMF exposure. The results have been replicated in multiple countries and continue to inform our understanding of EMF health effects.
