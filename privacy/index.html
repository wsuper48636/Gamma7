Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gamma-7 ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use this website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Order information, including products purchased and payment confirmation (payment card details are handled directly by PayPal — we never see or store them).
  • Messages you send us through the contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and customer support.
  • To send order confirmations and respond to enquiries.
  • To improve our website and product offering.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate this site, including PayPal for payment processing, Resend for transactional email, Supabase for secure order storage, and a spam-prevention (CAPTCHA) service on our contact form. Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

We retain order records for as long as necessary to comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations. Contact form messages are retained only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at support@gamma7.online.

6. Cookies

We use minimal local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember items in your shopping cart between pages. This data stays in your browser and is not shared with us until you check out.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact us or email support@gamma7.online.
